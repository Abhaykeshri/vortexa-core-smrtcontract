# M9 Audit Report: Zap

Date: 2026-10-28. Branch `milestone/M9` at `231f62e` plus the working-tree fixes described here.

Scope:
- `src/Zap.sol`, `src/interfaces/IZap.sol`, `src/interfaces/IZapDependencies.sol`, `src/libraries/NormalLPMath.sol` and `src/libraries/SwapMath.sol` (new);
- the M9 changes to `src/SwapExecutor.sol` (the floor moved to `SwapMath`, new `minOutFromReference`), `src/strategies/NormalLPStrategy.sol` (sizing moved to `NormalLPMath`) and `src/Vault.sol` (new `strategy()` getter), each checked to be behaviour-preserving;
- `script/DeployM9.s.sol`, `script/PostDeployM9Check.s.sol` and the `PostDeployM8Check` virtual hooks;
- the M9 tests;
- the Zap end to end against the real Uniswap v4 `PoolManager`, `PositionManager`, `StateView` and Universal Router path (in-process), and on a mainnet fork.

The guide references are sections 8.3, 11, 12.1, 13.3 and 13.4.

## Verdict

The Zap matches guide 11: no caller-chosen call target, swaps only through the executor with the oracle floor, exact and revoked allowances, balance-delta accounting, and refunds of call-local leftovers only. No path was found that loses user funds or lets one caller take another's.

Two Low defects are fixed and pinned by tests that fail on the original code:
- the split was computed from the raw token amounts of tiny stored liquidity (M9-01);
- `quoteZap` could promise a few raw units more than the zap mints, so a `minShares` set to the quote reverted (M9-02).

The M9 test suite had silent gaps (M9-03). A constructor test stopped after its first case, and four reverts were unpinned. The invariant swallowed every revert, and the post-deploy check had one negative test. Round 1 of mutation testing: 41 of 63 mutants of the Zap and the post-deploy check survived. After the new tests, every remaining survivor is equivalent or deliberate slack (see Verification).

The guide's fork sandwich simulation was missing (M9-04); it is added. The production ETH/USDC oracle tolerance (1,000 bps against the guide's 50) sets the zap's sandwich bound (M9-05); it is reported, not changed. The branch could not pass CI (M9-06): Slither failed on an unreviewed High false positive in the Zap, and `.gas-snapshot` had no entry for any M9 test; both are fixed.

## Behaviour reviewed and kept (documented in `docs/known-issues.md`)

- **The split refunds about `fee / 2` of the input.** It uses guide 8.3's formula (shared with the strategy, which the guide prescribes): it buys the whole token0 shortfall measured before the fee, so token1 ends short by the fee and the deposit refunds the excess token0. Measured 261 ppm on a 100 USDC zap in a 0.05% pool. The exact solution would split the fee across both legs; the guide accepts the remainder ("the split does not need to be exact"). Dropping the fee gross-up instead leaves the same amount in the other token (mutant `z-plan-fee` survives for that reason).
- **Large zaps into concentrated ladders refund more**, because the split is priced before the zap's own swap: 0.05% of a 1,000 USDC zap, 0.29% at 10,000 and about 1.1% at 40,000 in the test pool. On a first deposit the vault's own plan swaps the remainder (about 0.15% of a 10,000 USDC zap), so almost nothing is refunded.
- **A wrong-token zap can close a one-sided vault's window**: the zap's own swap can push the price into a ladder sitting at the price, and the deposit reverts with `DepositNotAllowed` atomically.
- **The Zap pins its three strategy contracts**, so a new strategy deployment needs a new Zap, executor and factory.
- **`quoteZap` does not check PriceGuard trust**, and dust zaps it quotes can still revert (`ZeroLiquidity`, `FirstDepositTooSmall`, or a few-unit swap that misses the 1% floor after rounding).
- **Anything sent to the Zap outside a zap stays there** (direct transfers, forced ETH, vault withdrawals or deposits naming the Zap as receiver). The snapshots keep it out of every call; there is no sweep.
- **The SwapExecutor, `NormalLPStrategy` and `Vault` changes are behaviour-preserving**: the floor and sizing are the same arithmetic moved into libraries; `strategy()` is a view. The M7 strategy and executor suites pass unchanged.
